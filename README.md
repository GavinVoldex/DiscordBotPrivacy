Privacy Policy

Bot: UF Last updated: August 27, 2026

This document explains what data the UF Discord bot collects, why it collects it, and how it is handled.

What the bot collects

UF only stores what it needs in order to function:

Discord user IDs — used to identify who ran a command and to store per-user settings or records.
Server (guild) IDs and channel IDs — used to store per-server configuration and to know where to send responses.
Command inputs — any text or options you pass to a command are processed to generate a response.
Message content — only read when required for a specific feature. The bot does not log or store general message history.
What the bot does not collect
Passwords, payment information, or email addresses.
Direct messages you send to other users.
Voice data.
Any data used for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.
Why the data is used

Collected data is used solely to operate the bot's features, such as remembering server settings, responding to commands, and preventing abuse. It is never sold or shared with third parties, except where required by law.

Storage and retention

Data is stored on infrastructure controlled by the bot operator and kept only as long as it is needed. If the bot is removed from a server, that server's stored configuration may be deleted.

Deleting your data

You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting the bot operator (see below). Removing the bot from your server will also stop any further collection.

Third-party services

The bot runs on the Discord platform and is subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy:
